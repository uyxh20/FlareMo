# Month, quarter and year review

A period review is the weekly review over a whole month, quarter or year: the same steps (opening, coach replies, scores, memo), the same answer shapes, with the period's material and words. Read "week" in the step messages as the period when they talk about the period under review.

## Focus

1. **Focus, read whole first.** `review_period` for the period: its goals and results, every week of it with its scores, verdict and saved weekly summary, its diary, and the health totals. Every week of the period is in focus.
2. **Near background.** The period's own weeks in full with `week_diary` when a summary and the diary disagree: the diary wins. The period before it, through `review_period` or `list_entries`, when you are about to say something changed since.
3. **Far background.** Everything older through `list_entries` and `search_entries`, never read whole.

Weekly summaries were written by a model, sometimes from a cut-down diary. Treat them as claims to check against that week's diary, never as evidence, and never quote them as the owner's words.

## The opening's "what happened"

The opening says what happened in the period claim by claim, each with the owner's own words it rests on and the day. The server looks for every quote word for word in the period's rows and drops the claims it cannot find, so copy exactly and leave out a claim you cannot quote.

## The memo

Fill the skeleton the memo step carries, exactly, under its rules, with the same standard as the weekly memo: specific days and numbers, the owner's words quoted, a pattern only across named weeks, gaps stated not filled, plain and direct. Check every concrete claim against what you read before you write the file. Then write the memo where the step says, add a few lines about this period to your memory store, and make the memory proposals the step allows (at most three, `read_memories` first, each with the owner's words as the receipt). Reply `done`.

A period review proposes no goals or tasks.

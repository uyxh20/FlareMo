Search and vectors

Two separate searches. Keyword search matches your exact words. Semantic search matches meaning, using vectors.

Memory and dreaming

Memories are short conclusions about you, kept apart from the diary so AI agents don't have to reread everything.

Weekly review fork

Look back chats about the week and writes a summary. Look forward turns goals into a plan. Weekends review this week, weekdays last week. A week runs Monday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 in your own time (Copenhagen), so a note written at 00:30 on Monday counts for the new week.

Month, quarter and year reviews fork

The weekly review's superset. A period closes on the Sunday of its last week (a week belongs to the month of its Thursday). That Sunday stays one sitting: Look back, then the period review in the middle, then Look forward against the new goals. Five steps per level, the same shape as the weekly: raw rows on the right, your words and a reader on the left, a memo you edit, Save. The model reads, checks, counts and quotes; goals, verdicts and marks are yours. Design: docs/planning-cockpit-period-review.md. On dev since 2026-10-17; not on live.

Cockpit and task history fork

One board on top of FlareMo's tasks, and a history log that outlives the tasks themselves.

Goals fork

Five levels, each split across four pillars. Tasks point at the goal they serve.

Health tracker fork

Live since 2026-10-13 (version 17). Nothing new to type: every saved entry is read for training, breathwork and nights out, and the results become rows. No AI; fixed word rules.
